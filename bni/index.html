When you hear one of these, think of me

The best referrals come from everyday conversations. Any of these lines means someone needs a protection check.

  • We just booked a flat, the home loan came through.

    Term cover sized to the loan, so the family keeps the home if the earning member isn't there.

  • We're hiring, the team is growing.

    Group health cover for employees, and keyman cover for the people the business can't run without.

  • We're expecting our first baby.

    Family floater health cover and a plan for the child's education, started early.

  • My parents are turning sixty.

    Senior citizen health cover, which is easier to get before a medical issue comes up.

  • I'm retiring in a couple of years.

    Retirement and pension planning, so the monthly income continues after the salary stops.

  • Just bought a new car.

    Motor insurance with the right add-ons, and a quick look at their other cover while we talk.
