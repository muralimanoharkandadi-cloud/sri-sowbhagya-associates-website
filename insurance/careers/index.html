How you get started

A structured path from interest to your first policy sold.

  1. Register your interest

    Tell us a little about yourself using the form below, or on WhatsApp. We'll call you to understand your background and goals.

  2. Complete pre-licensing training

    IRDAI requires a mandated number of training hours before you can sit the licensing exam. We guide you through approved training resources.

  3. Clear the IRDAI licensing exam

    We help you prepare with practice material and mock sessions based on the current exam pattern.

  4. Get onboarded with our partner insurers

    Once licensed, we help you complete paperwork with our partners, including Tata AIA, Tata AIG, Care Health and Motilal Oswal.

  5. Start with mentored support

    Your first few client conversations are supported directly, so you're never selling a policy you don't fully understand.
