Mutual Fund & Wealth Building Strategies

Disciplined portfolio options aligned with your horizon and risk profile.

Industry snapshot (AMFI data, June 2026): India’s mutual fund industry AUM stood at ₹82.22 lakh crore, with SIP AUM at ₹17.70 lakh crore across 9.78 crore active SIP accounts, and monthly SIP contributions of ₹31,781 crore. Figures change monthly — shown here for context, not as a return projection.

Systematic Investment Plans (SIP)

Automated, rupee-cost-averaging investment pathways designed for disciplined long-term wealth accumulation. You invest a fixed amount at regular intervals (typically monthly), buying more units when prices are low and fewer when high — smoothing out market volatility over time.

  • Start from as low as ₹500/month in most schemes
  • SIP AUM has more than tripled between 2021 and 2026, now ~20% of total industry AUM (AMFI-Crisil study, March 2026)
  • Can be paused, stepped-up, or stopped at any time

Equity Mutual Funds

Capital appreciation strategies focused on large-cap, mid-cap, multi-cap, flexi-cap, and sector-oriented businesses. Historically the highest-growth category, but also the most volatile in the short term.

  • Best suited for goals 5+ years away
  • Sub-categories: Large-Cap, Mid-Cap, Small-Cap, Flexi-Cap, Sectoral/Thematic, ELSS (tax-saving, 3-year lock-in)
  • Flexi-Cap and Mid-Cap funds drew the largest net inflows through 2026 per AMFI monthly data

Debt / Fixed Income Funds

Capital preservation instruments investing in government securities, corporate bonds, and money market instruments — offering lower volatility and steadier, more predictable income than equity.

  • Suited for short-to-medium term goals or capital protection
  • Sub-categories: Liquid, Short Duration, Corporate Bond, Gilt Funds
  • Carries interest-rate and credit risk, though generally lower than equity market risk

Hybrid Asset Allocation

Balanced funds dynamically combining equity growth with debt stability in a single scheme — aiming to moderate volatility while still participating in market upside.

  • Sub-categories: Aggressive Hybrid, Conservative Hybrid, Balanced Advantage, Multi-Asset Allocation
  • Useful for investors wanting one fund covering both growth and stability
