      How this plan is worked out
      • Base cover starts from typical hospital costs in your city type: ₹10 lakh for a metro like Hyderabad, ₹7.5 lakh for other large cities, ₹5 lakh for smaller towns.
      • A family floater adds 50% for a second adult and 25% per child, then moves up one level if the eldest adult is 45 or older.
      • Parents aged around 60 and above are usually better covered by a separate senior citizen policy, so their claims do not use up the family floater.
      • A super top-up adds large cover at a lower premium. It pays after a claim crosses the deductible, which we set equal to your base cover.
      • Amounts are rounded to the cover levels insurers typically offer.
