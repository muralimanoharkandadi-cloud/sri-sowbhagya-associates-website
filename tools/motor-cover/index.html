      How this estimate is worked out
      • IDV is the most the insurer pays if the vehicle is stolen or a total loss. It is the ex-showroom price minus depreciation for the vehicle's age.
      • Depreciation follows the standard motor tariff schedule: 5% under 6 months, 15% up to 1 year, 20% up to 2 years, 30% up to 3 years, 40% up to 4 years and 50% up to 5 years.
      • For vehicles older than 5 years, IDV is agreed with the insurer based on condition, so the range shown is only a starting point.
      • No claim bonus (NCB) is a discount on the own-damage premium: 20%, 25%, 35%, 45% and 50% for 1 to 5+ claim-free years.
      • Third-party liability cover is compulsory under the Motor Vehicles Act for every vehicle on the road.
