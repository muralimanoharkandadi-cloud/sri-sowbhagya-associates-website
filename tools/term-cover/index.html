      How this estimate is worked out
      • Income replacement: your annual income multiplied by an age-based factor (20× up to age 30, 15× for 31 to 40, 12× for 41 to 50, 8× above 50), limited to the years left until retirement with a minimum of 5 years.
      • Loans and future goals are added, so your family is not left with debt or unfunded commitments.
      • Existing personal life cover and usable savings are subtracted.
      • The result is rounded up to the nearest ₹25 lakh, which is how term cover is usually offered.
      • This is a planning guide. Insurers decide the maximum cover you qualify for based on income proof, age, health and lifestyle.
